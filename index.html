About Valda Solutions

We provide hotels and institutions with their dry foods needs. We also offer housekeeping and cleaning materials.

Valda Solutions Limited is a Kenyan FMCG solutions provider. Procurement and kitchen teams usually deal with several suppliers for food, cleaning products and disposables. We bring those into one relationship, so ordering, delivery and invoicing are handled in one place.

We work with organisations that buy in volume and need the same goods, on time, every cycle. Our job is to make that routine for them.

Questions procurement teams ask

Do you have a minimum order?

Minimums depend on the product and pack size. Send your list and we will confirm quantities with the quotation.

Can I supply my own purchase list or LPO?

Yes. Send your list or LPO by WhatsApp or email and we will quote against it.

Where do you deliver?

Tell us your delivery location in the request and we will confirm coverage and the delivery date.

What are your payment terms?

Terms are agreed per account when the first order is confirmed. Ask us for the details when you request a quote.

Can you set up regular supply?

Yes. Many institutions restock weekly or monthly, and we can quote a standing order for the same list.

Can you send your company documents?

Yes. The supplier registration pack above is available on request.

Ready to restock?

Send your list and we will quote your quantities and delivery location. Call or WhatsApp 0724 168 922.
